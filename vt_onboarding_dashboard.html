Step 1: Register Your Account

⏱️ 5 minutes Not Started
▼
1.1 Fill Basic Information
What You Need:
  • Valid email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • 5 minutes of time

Fields to Complete: Account Type, First Name, Last Name, Country, Phone, Email

1.2 Complete Personal Details
Important: All names must match your ID documents (Passport/IC)
  • Title, First/Middle/Last Name
  • Nationality, Date of Birth (18+ required)
  • Place of Birth
  • ID Type & Number
  • Referral Code (optional)
1.3 Fill Residential Address
Required Fields:
  • Country of Main Residency
  • Full street address with unit number
  • Province/State, City/Suburb, Postcode
  • US Tax Residency Question (Select "No" unless applicable)
1.4 Complete Financial Survey
Why Required: AML/CTF compliance - regulators require this information
  • Employment Status
  • Estimated Annual Income
  • Estimated Savings & Investments
  • Intended Deposit Amount
  • Source of Funds
  • Trading Knowledge & Experience
1.5 Choose Account Configuration
Important Decisions:
  • Platform: MetaTrader 4 (MT4) - Most compatible with trading robots
  • Account Type: Standard STP vs BEBAS SWAP (7-day Islamic trial)
  • Currency: USD recommended for stability

Recommended Configuration

  • Platform: MT4 (best EA support, simpler interface)
  • Account Type: BEBAS SWAP (7-day free trial, then converts to Islamic)
  • Currency: USD (most stable, globally accepted)
Pro Tips for Registration
  • Have your ID document ready before starting
  • Use a valid email you check regularly
  • Choose BEBAS SWAP to test swap-free trading (converts after 7 days)
  • You can create multiple accounts later for different strategies
  • Save your login credentials immediately after receiving them
📄

Step 2: Verify Your Identity

⏱️ 5 minutes Not Started
▼
2.1 Access Verification Portal
Two Methods:
  • Method 1: Click "Verification status" link in your registration email
  • Method 2: Login to Client Portal → Profile icon → "Verification status"
2.2 Prepare Required Documents
Proof of Identity (choose ONE):
  • 📸 National ID Card (front + back photos)
  • 🛂 Passport (photo page + signature page)
Proof of Residency (choose ONE):
  • 💡 Utility Bill (electricity, water, gas) - within 3 months
  • 🏦 Bank Statement - within 3 months
  • 📄 Residence Certificate - within 3 months
2.3 Upload Documents
Photo Requirements:
  • ✅ Clear, readable text
  • ✅ All 4 corners visible
  • ✅ No glare or shadows
  • ✅ Color photos (not black & white)
  • ✅ File formats: JPG, PNG, PDF
  • ✅ Max file size: 5MB per file
2.4 Wait for Approval
Verification Statuses:
  • 🔴 Incomplete: You haven't finished uploading documents
  • 🟡 Processing: Documents being reviewed (15 min - 24 hours)
  • 🟢 Verified: ✅ Approved! You can now deposit and trade

Typical Processing Time: 15 minutes to 24 hours

Pro Tips for Verification
  • Take photos in good lighting to avoid glare
  • Ensure ALL document corners are visible in the frame
  • For proof of address, utility bills are fastest (TNB, water, internet)
  • Document must be dated within last 3 months
  • Address on document must match your registration address
  • Keep originals handy in case re-upload is needed
💵

Step 3: Deposit Funds

⏱️ 5 minutes Not Started
▼
3.1 Access Deposit Page
How to Access:
  • Login to Client Portal
  • Click "Deposit funds" button on center dashboard
  • View all available payment options
3.2 Choose Payment Method
Available Methods:
  • ⭐ FPX (Malaysia): Instant, $0 fees, 16 banks supported
  • 💳 Credit/Debit Card: Instant, $0 fees, global
  • 📱 E-Wallets: Instant, multiple options
  • 🍎 Apple/Google Pay: Instant, secure
  • 🏦 Bank Transfer: 2-5 days, no limits
  • ₿ Cryptocurrency: ~1 hour, 19+ coins

Minimum Deposit: $20 USD

3.3 Complete Payment
Payment Process:
  • Enter deposit amount (min $20)
  • Fill in payment details
  • Complete authentication (OTP/PIN)
  • Receive confirmation

FPX Exchange Rate: RM4.10193 = $1.00 USD (approx)

3.4 Verify Funds Received
After Payment:
  • Check your Client Portal dashboard
  • FPX/Card: Balance updates instantly
  • Bank Transfer: Wait 1-5 business days
  • Crypto: Wait ~1 hour for blockchain confirmation

Keep your transaction receipt for records!

Recommended Payment Method

  • For Malaysian Traders: FPX (Instant, $0 fees, 24/7)
  • For International: Credit/Debit Card (Instant, $0 fees)
  • For Large Deposits: Bank Transfer (No limits, secure)
Pro Tips for Deposits
  • Use FPX for fastest deposits if in Malaysia
  • Start with minimum $20 to test the process
  • Deposit during business hours for faster processing
  • Always keep transaction receipts
  • All deposit methods have $0 fees from VT Markets
  • Contact support via Live Chat if deposit takes >24 hours
